1. Buildrite Sapal RTU with Polyurethane
Hybrid Sealant
Ready-to-use elastomeric sealant reinforced with polyurethane for perimeter sealing and crack bridging.
  • Application: Window perimeter blockouts, flashing joints, parapet coping junctions.
  • Substrate Prep: Joint must be clean, free from dust, oil, and unbonded cement laitance.
  • Method: Apply primer if porous; tool joint smoothly within 15 minutes of gunning.
  • Cure Time: Skin forming 30 mins; full depth curing 2-3 mm per 24 hours.
2. Buildrite Confix / Smartcrete Concrete Repair
Structural Repair
Polymer-modified non-shrink repair mortar for structural patching of tie-rod holes, honeycombs, and spalls.
  • Chipping: Chip perimeter square to minimum 15mm depth around rebar to remove weak zones.
  • Bonding Slurry: Apply Smartbond latex bonding agent wet-on-wet.
  • Troweling: Place Confix in lifts up to 30mm; compact thoroughly without voids.
  • Strength: >40 MPa compressive strength at 28 days.
3. Buildrite Stopgap Polyurethane Sealant
Expansion Joints
High performance low-modulus moisture-curing PU sealant for facade and building expansion joints.
  • Joint Geometry: Width-to-depth ratio 2:1 (minimum 6mm width, 6mm depth).
  • Backer Rod: Install closed-cell polyethylene backing rod compressed 25%.
  • Movement: ±25% joint movement capability. Paintable once fully cured.
4. Buildrite Floortek Self-Leveling Screed
Floor Leveling
Rapid-hardening polymer-modified underlayment screed for vinyl, wood, and tile flooring substrates.
  • Priming: Apply Floortek Primer to suppress air pinholes and improve adhesion.
  • Pouring: Mix with exact water ratio; pour continuously; roll with spike roller to de-aerate.
  • Thickness: 3mm to 15mm single pour. Light foot traffic ready in 4 hours.

1. Davies Sun & Rain Elastomeric Waterproof Paint
Elastomeric Barrier
100% acrylic elastomeric wall coating that provides superior weather protection and bridges exterior hairline cracks.
  • Features: Elongation up to 300%; shields against wind-driven tropical rainfall.
  • Primer: 1 coat Davies Sun & Rain diluted with max 10% clean water.
  • Topcoat: 2 undiluted coats applied by brush, roller, or airless spray.
  • Dry Time: 2 hours between coats; 24 hours full film cure.
2. Davies Megacryl 100% Acrylic Latex
Interior / Exterior Finish
Low-VOC, low-odor 100% acrylic emulsion paint offering excellent scrub and fungal resistance.
  • Primer: Davies 1350 Megacryl Concrete Primer & Sealer.
  • Putty: Davies 5000 Megacryl Masonry Putty for dry interior walls.
  • Topcoat: 2 coats Davies Megacryl (Flat, Semi-Gloss, or Gloss).
3. Davies Powerfloor Epoxy Floor System
Epoxy Floor Coating
Two-component polyamide cured epoxy protective coating for parking decks, ramps, and plant floors.
  • Surface Prep: Diamond grind slab to CSP 2-3; remove oil, grease, and weak laitance.
  • Primer: 1 coat Davies Eposeal Clear Epoxy Primer (3:1 mix).
  • Topcoat: 2 coats Davies Powerfloor Heavy Duty Topcoat; pot life 4 hrs.
4. Davies Keratech Liquid Tile System
Architectural Texture
High-durability acrylic solvent textured coating for high-traffic corridors and exterior building facades.
  • Undercoat: Keratech Cast Primer applied evenly by roller.
  • Texture Coat: Applied with hopper spray gun or textured sponge roller to match approved sample board.
  • Sealer: Topcoated with Keratech clear or pigmented glaze for UV protection.
