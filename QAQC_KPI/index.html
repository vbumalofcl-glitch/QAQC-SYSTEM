📖 Multi-KPI Normalization & Rating Methodology

In construction QA/QC, field measurements operate in disparate, incompatible units (FTQ is in %, Rework is in ₱ currency, Defect Density is in items/100m², NCR Velocity is in calendar days, and Punch Lists are in snags). The Multi-KPI Normalization Engine converts every field measurement into a uniform Normalized Quality Score (\(S \in [0, 100]\)).

1. Standard 0–100 Scale Normalization
100.0 Score
Flawless Benchmark: Represents theoretical zero-defect perfection (\(0.00\%\) rework, \(0.0\) defects, on-time clearance).
0.0 Score
Critical Breach: Represents severe non-compliance triggering billing holds or turnover blockage.
2. The 5 Quality Pillars & Constant Weighting (20% Each)
\[ \text{Corporate Quality Index} = \frac{S_{\text{FTQ}} + S_{\text{Rework}} + S_{\text{Defects}} + S_{\text{NCR}} + S_{\text{Punch}}}{5} \]
20% Weight
Equal Axis Distribution: Ensures equal balance across Execution (FTQ), Financial Discipline (Rework), Detection Rigor (Defects), Engineering Responsiveness (NCR), and Handover Readiness (Punch).
3. Established Pass/Fail Benchmark Matrix
KPI 1: FTQ Pass Rate ≥ 95.0% (Score 95)
KPI 2: Rework Rate ≤ 2.00% (Score 50)
KPI 3: Defect Density ≤ 2.00/100m² (Score 60)
KPI 4: NCR Turnaround ≤ 5–7 Days (Score 70)
KPI 5: Punch Clearance 100% (0 Critical)
4. Corporate Performance Tiers
Tier A (≥ 90.0%): World-Class Quality Excellence (Pass / Benchmark Project).
Tier B (75.0% – 89.9%): Controlled Compliance (Stable operations / standard monitoring).
Tier C (60.0% – 74.9%): Moderate Risk (Requires formal Quality Action Plan).
Tier D (< 60.0%): Critical Risk (Executive Intervention & Progress Billing Hold).

3. KPI 1: First-Time Pass Rate (FTQ %) Dual-Axis Combo Chart Comprehension

Volume of testing rigor (Left Y-Axis) vs Right-First-Time execution pass rate (Right Y-Axis)

Target: ≥ 90.0% – 95.0%

📐 Visual Anatomy Callouts

  • 🔵 Left Y-Axis (Test Volume Bars): Blue bars represent Passed Tests; Red bars represent Failed Tests. The total bar height represents sample size and testing intensity.
  • 🟢 Right Y-Axis (FTQ Rate Line): Emerald line tracks the percentage of tests passing on their first attempt without re-testing or rework.
  • 🔴 Red Dashed Benchmark: Set at 90.0%. Points falling below this line are flagged with red circular markers indicating systemic non-compliance requiring an immediate root-cause investigation.

📖 KPI 1: Rating Computation & Constant Values Explained

1. Scoring Formula
\[ \text{FTQ Pass Rate (\%)} = \left( \frac{\text{Passed Field Tests}}{\text{Total Tests Conducted}} \right) \times 100\% \] \[ \text{Score}_{\text{FTQ}} = \max(0, \min(100, \text{Raw FTQ})) \]

Measures the proportion of quality and material compliance tests (concrete compressive cylinders, rebar tensile yield, compaction density, weld NDT, hydrostatic pressure) that pass on their first attempt without requiring re-testing or structural patching.

2. Constant Values & Established Rationale
100% Baseline
Flawless First-Time Execution: Direct 1:1 score mapping where 100 represents zero testing failures.
≥ 95.0% Target Constant
Structural Design Code Compliance: Mandated by ACI 318 / NSCP 2015 / ASTM C39, allowing at most a 5% statistical margin for material variance. Yields a minimum compliance score of 95.0 / 100.
< 90.0% Critical Floor
Hold-Point Trigger: Any project falling below 90.0% indicates systemic concrete batching or material supplier defects, triggering a mandatory QA/QC Stop-Pour audit.
💡 Step-by-Step Practical Example
• Field Data: 28 passed concrete cylinder tests out of 30 total tests conducted.
• Raw FTQ Calculation: (28 / 30) × 100 = 93.33%
• Score Derivation: Direct 1:1 scale gives Score = 93.3 / 100  →  TOLERABLE MARGIN (Review Sub-trade Batches)

4. KPI 2: Quality Rework BOQ Breakdown & Cumulative Trend Comprehension

Itemized Rework Breakdown Rate (Left Y-Axis) vs Accumulated Project Rework Cost (Right Y-Axis)

Target: ≤ 1.00% – 2.00%

📐 Visual Anatomy Callouts

  • 🟢 Green Bar (≤ 1.00%): Optimal controlled rework. Rework expense is within standard industry contingency buffers.
  • 🟡 Amber Bar (1.00% – 2.00%): Controlled margin. Approaching maximum corporate limit.
  • 🔴 Red Bar (> 2.00%): Threshold breached. Individual rework cost significantly degrades BOQ trade profit margins.
  • 🔵 Blue Polyline (Accumulated Project Rate): Tracks overall project cumulative rework expenditure divided by the total approved contract budget over time.

📖 KPI 2: Rating Computation & Constant Values Explained

1. Scoring Formula
\[ \text{Rework Rate (\%)} = \left( \frac{\text{Grand Total Rework Cost}}{\text{Approved BOQ Item Cost}} \right) \times 100\% \] \[ \text{Score}_{\text{Rework}} = \max(0, \min(100, 100 - (\text{Rework Rate (\%)} \times 25))) \]

Measures the financial loss resulting from defective workmanship, demolished cast-in-place members, and ruined materials that must be rectified at developer/contractor expense, calculated specifically against the Approved BOQ Item Contract Cost Baseline.

2. Constant Values & Established Rationale
100 (Base Score)
Pristine Baseline: Zero rework incurred (\(0.00\%\) rework rate, ₱0 financial loss) earns the maximum score of 100.0 / 100.
Multiplier 25
Penalty Gradient Factor: The corporate maximum allowable tolerance limit for quality rework is 2.00% of the approved BOQ item value. Multiplying by 25 ensures that reaching the maximum limit of \(2.00\%\) rework incurs an exact 50-point penalty (\(2.00 \times 25 = 50.0\)), landing directly on the 50.0% Pass/Fail Threshold:
\[ \text{At } 2.00\% \text{ Rework: } 100 - (2.00 \times 25) = 100 - 50.0 = 50.0 \text{ (Pass Threshold)} \]
Every \(0.10\%\) of rework incurs a \(2.5\)-point score deduction.
≤ 1.00% Target
Optimal Contingency Buffer: Yields \(100 - (1.00 \times 25) = \mathbf{75.0 / 100}\) (industry standard profit margin protection).
≥ 4.00% Zero Floor
Total Margin Collapse: Any severe rework exceeding \(4.00\%\) results in \(100 - (4.00 \times 25) = \mathbf{0.0 / 100}\), reflecting total trade margin collapse.
💡 Step-by-Step Practical Example
• Field Data: Project incurs ₱125,000 in rework on an approved BOQ item budget of ₱15,000,000.
• Raw Rework Rate: (₱125,000 / ₱15,000,000) × 100 = 0.83%
• Constant Multiplier Applied: 0.83 × 25 = 20.75 penalty points
• Score Derivation: 100 - 20.75 = 79.25 ≈ 79.2 / 100  →  PASS (Optimal Controlled Rework)

5. KPI 3: Defect Density Dual-Axis Combo Chart Comprehension

Inspected Surface Area (Left Y-Axis) vs Normalized Defect Density per 100 m² (Right Y-Axis)

Target: ≤ 2.00 Defects / 100 m²

📐 Visual Anatomy Callouts

  • 🔵 Left Y-Axis (Inspected Floor / Facade Area): Bars show the total square meters surveyed during each quality audit. Larger bars represent comprehensive floor-by-floor inspections.
  • 🟢 Right Y-Axis (Defect Density Line): Tracks defects per \(100\text{ m}^2\), normalizing defect counts across zones of differing dimensions.
  • 🔴 Threshold Breaches: Data points rising above the \(2.00\) ceiling are highlighted in red to signal high defect frequency in specific trade packages (e.g. architectural finishes, concrete spalling).

📖 KPI 3: Rating Computation & Constant Values Explained

1. Scoring Formula
\[ \text{Defect Density} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Defects Found}}{\text{Total Inspected Area (m}^2\text{)}} \right) \times 100 \] \[ \text{Score}_{\text{Defects}} = \max(0, \min(100, 100 - (\text{Defect Density} \times 20))) \]

Measures the frequency of physical workmanship defects (honeycombs, cold joints, plaster hollows, paint blisters, tile lippage, MEP rough-in misalignments) normalized per 100 square meters (\(100\text{ m}^2\)) of inspected surface area.

2. Constant Values & Established Rationale
100 (in Density)
Area-Invariant Multiplier: Standardizes defect counts to a constant \(100\text{ m}^2\) area grid, ensuring equal, unbiased comparison between a \(500\text{ m}^2\) podium zone and a \(5,000\text{ m}^2\) floor plate.
100 (Base Score)
Zero-Defect Baseline: Zero defects detected across all inspected surfaces (\(0.0\text{ defects}/100\text{ m}^2 = 100.0/100\)) earns the maximum score of 100.0 / 100.
Multiplier 20
Defect Penalty Coefficient: The corporate upper acceptable ceiling for defect density is \(2.00\text{ defects} / 100\text{ m}^2\). Multiplying by 20 scales the maximum threshold of \(2.00\) into exactly \(40\) penalty points (\(2.00 \times 20 = 40.0\)), landing directly on the 60.0% Pass Threshold:
\[ \text{At } 2.00\text{ Defects}/100\text{ m}^2: 100 - (2.00 \times 20) = 100 - 40.0 = 60.0 \text{ (Pass Threshold)} \]
Every \(1.0\text{ defect}/100\text{ m}^2\) deducts \(20\) points from the quality score.
≥ 5.00 Zero Floor
Severe Density Breach: Any defect density \(\ge 5.00\text{ defects}/100\text{ m}^2\) reduces the score to \(100 - (5.0 \times 20) = \mathbf{0.0 / 100}\).
💡 Step-by-Step Practical Example
• Field Data: 18 defects discovered across \(1,200\text{ m}^2\) of inspected floor area.
• Raw Defect Density: (18 / 1,200) × 100 = 1.50 defects / 100 m²
• Constant Multiplier Applied: 1.50 × 20 = 30.0 penalty points
• Score Derivation: 100 - 30.0 = 70.0 / 100  →  PASS (Compliant Workmanship)

6. KPI 4: NCR Resolution Velocity Line Graph Comprehension

Turnaround Duration in Days vs Issue Date Timeline against 5–7 Day Target SLA

Target: ≤ 5.0 – 7.0 Days

📐 Visual Anatomy Callouts

  • 🔵 Line & Node Points: Each point represents an issued Non-Conformance Report (NCR) plotted against its date of issuance.
  • 🟢 Green Nodes (≤ 5 Days): Resolved rapidly within optimal target timeframe.
  • 🔴 Red Nodes (> 7 Days): Breached maximum threshold. Indicates subcontractor non-responsiveness or slow engineering rectification.

📖 KPI 4: Rating Computation & Constant Values Explained

1. Scoring Formula
\[ \overline{D}_{\text{NCR}} = \frac{1}{N_{\text{closed}}} \sum_{k=1}^{N_{\text{closed}}} (\text{Date Closed}_k - \text{Date Issued}_k) \] \[ \text{Score}_{\text{NCR}} = \begin{cases} 100.0 & \text{if } \overline{D}_{\text{NCR}} \le 5.0\text{ Days} \\ \max(0, \min(100, 100 - ((\overline{D}_{\text{NCR}} - 5.0) \times 15))) & \text{if } \overline{D}_{\text{NCR}} > 5.0\text{ Days} \end{cases} \]

Measures the turnaround speed (in calendar days) from when an official Non-Conformance Report (NCR) is issued until the contractor completes physical rectification, QA/QC conducts reinspection, and the NCR is formally closed.

2. Constant Values & Established Rationale
5.0 Days SLA
Target Grace Benchmark: Resolving NCRs within 5.0 calendar days incurs zero penalty and earns the maximum score of 100.0 / 100.
(\(\overline{D} - 5.0\))
Resolution Delay Days: Measures only the overdue delay days exceeding the 5.0-day target SLA.
Multiplier 15
Turnaround Lag Penalty Multiplier: The corporate upper tolerance limit for closing non-conformances is 7.0 Days (a 2.0-day delay past the 5-day SLA). Multiplying delay days by 15 produces an exact 30-point penalty (\((7.0 - 5.0) \times 15 = 2.0 \times 15 = 30.0\)), landing directly on the 70.0% Pass Threshold:
\[ \text{At } 7.0\text{ Days: } 100 - ((7.0 - 5.0) \times 15) = 100 - 30.0 = 70.0 \text{ (Pass Threshold)} \]
Every single day of delay beyond 5 days subtracts \(15\) points from the contractor's rating.
14.0 Days Limit
Commercial Sanction Rule: Open NCRs exceeding 14 calendar days trigger an automatic financial hold on monthly progress billing certifications.
💡 Step-by-Step Practical Example
• Field Data: Average turnaround cycle time across closed NCRs is \(6.2\text{ calendar days}\).
• SLA Delay Days: 6.2 - 5.0 = 1.2 delay days
• Constant Multiplier Applied: 1.2 × 15 = 18.0 penalty points
• Score Derivation: 100 - 18.0 = 82.0 / 100  →  PASS (Responsive Engineering Rectification)

7. KPI 5: Handover Punch List Clearance, Burndown & Target Clearance Schedule

Triple View Modes: Snag Burndown Trajectory vs Discipline Breakdown vs Target Due Date Schedule

Target: 100% On-Time (0 Critical)

📐 View Mode 1 & View Mode 3 Visual Anatomy

  • 📉 View Mode 1 (Burndown Trend): Plots 🔵 Blue Line (Items Issued), 🟢 Dashed Green Line (Planned Clearance Pace), 🟡 Amber Line (Open Minor Snags), and 🔴 Red Line (Open Critical Snags — must reach 0).
  • 📊 View Mode 2 (Discipline Breakdown): Stacked bar chart showing Closed (🟢), In-Progress (🔵), and Open (🔴) snags across the 6 engineering trades.
  • 📅 View Mode 3 (Target Clearance Schedule): Dual-axis chart plotting Expected Items Due on each Target Date (stacked by 🟢 Cleared On-Time, 🟡 Pending, 🔴 Overdue) overlaid with the Cumulative Planned vs. Actual Clearance S-Curve polylines.

📖 KPI 5: Rating Computation & Constant Values Explained

1. Scoring Formula
\[ \text{On-Time Clearance Rate (\%)} = \left( \frac{N_{\text{total}} - N_{\text{overdue}}}{N_{\text{total}}} \right) \times 100\% \] \[ \text{Score}_{\text{Punch}} = \begin{cases} \max(60, \min(100, \text{On-Time \%})) & \text{if } N_{\text{critical open}} = 0 \\ \max(0, \min(50, \text{On-Time \%} - (N_{\text{critical open}} \times 25))) & \text{if } N_{\text{critical open}} > 0 \end{cases} \]

Measures pre-turnover snag clearance velocity against committed target dates, governed by a strict zero-tolerance gatekeeper rule for open critical snags.

2. Constant Values & Established Rationale
0 Critical Gate
Zero Critical Condition: When all structural, life-safety MEP, and water ingress snags are 100% resolved (\(N_{\text{crit}} = 0\)), the score reflects pure on-time clearance velocity (\(\ge 75.0\%\) pass).
50.0% Hard Cap
Gatekeeper Failure Limit: If even a single critical snag remains open on handover day, the project score is instantly capped at a maximum of 50.0% (Automatic Fail / Turnover Blocked), regardless of having 99% minor snags cleared.
Multiplier 25
Critical Snag Penalty Deductor: Each unresolved critical snag deducts an additional 25 points below the 50% cap:
  • 1 Open Critical Snag: \(\max(0, \min(50, 100 - (1 \times 25))) = \mathbf{\le 25.0 / 100}\)
  • 2+ Open Critical Snags: \(\mathbf{0.0 / 100}\) (Immediate project red-flag)
💡 Step-by-Step Practical Example
• Scenario A (Clean Turnover): 95% on-time clearance with 0 Open Critical Snags: Score = 95.0 / 100  →  TURNOVER APPROVED

📖 Variance Engine: Rating Computation & Polarity Methodology

1. Variance Mathematical Formulations
\[ \text{Absolute Shift } (\Delta) = \text{Actual}_{\text{Target Quarter}} - \text{Actual}_{\text{Base Quarter}} \] \[ \text{Relative Shift } (\Delta\%) = \left( \frac{\text{Actual}_{\text{Target}} - \text{Actual}_{\text{Base}}}{\text{Actual}_{\text{Base}}} \right) \times 100\% \]

\(\Delta\) evaluates the raw volume shift in underlying field units (%, ₱, items, days), while \(\Delta\%\) measures the rate of acceleration or deceleration between compared quarters.

2. Directional Polarity Rules (Why Color Logic Inverts)
Positive Polarity
Higher is Better (KPI 1 FTQ & KPI 5 Punch): \(+\Delta\) indicates 🟢 Favorable Improvement (Gain); \(-\Delta\) indicates 🔴 Unfavorable Deterioration (Drop).
Negative Polarity
Lower is Better (KPI 2 Rework, KPI 3 Defects, KPI 4 NCR Days): \(-\Delta\) indicates 🟢 Favorable Cost Savings & Speed; \(+\Delta\) indicates 🔴 Unfavorable Bleed & Delay.
3. Portfolio Quality Ranking Algorithm
Ranking Scale
Cross-project performance is indexed into 4 discrete management tiers: Tier A (\(\ge 90\%\)), Tier B (75%–89%), Tier C (60%–74%), and Tier D (< 60%).
